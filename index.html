Insider Tips & Tricks

1. Pelvic Neutrality on Thrusts & Squats

Avoid hyperextending the lumbar spine at the top of barbell hip thrusts. Keep chin tucked toward your chest and ribcage down (Pilates core engagement) to maximize gluteus maximus torque without lower back stress.

2. Diaphragmatic Breath & Transverse Abdominis

Inhale through the nose into the back and sides of the ribs on the eccentric (lowering phase). Exhale forcefully through pursed lips on the concentric phase (lifting) while pulling the navel to spine.

3. Time Under Tension (3-1-1 Tempo)

For optimal hypertrophy, control the lowering phase for 3 seconds, hold a 1-second squeeze at peak contraction, and return in 1 second.

4. Active Rest & Recovery

2 days each week are non-negotiable active recovery days. Performing 20-30 minutes of Mat Pilates mobility work improves hip joint alignment and speeds up gluteal recovery between heavy gym sessions.
